When does the winner move?

Adding ungraded tracks leaves the pin in place. Adding grades 4–5 brings a ground track within 4.278 km of that pin. The winner moves 336 m west-northwest, and the maximum falls to 4.599 km.

How we found it

We searched 16,333 km² of Styrian land, counting roads across the border. Each rule ran independently and bounded the best possible distance to within one metre. Only then did we compare the published pin, 214 m from our default winner.

What counts as a road?

The model asks where a forestry pickup could drive in snow-free conditions. Private roads and gated tracks can count. OSM smoothness takes priority over track grade. The distance measures a straight line. The terrain and the legal access needed to reach the pin are separate questions.
